# Incident response process

## Severity definitions

SEV1 means customer payments are failing or money is at risk. SEV2 means degraded service without payment loss. SEV3 means internal or cosmetic impact only.

## Declaring an incident

Any engineer may declare. Open a channel named #inc-<service>, post the symptoms, and assign an incident commander before beginning mitigation.

## Post-mortem requirement

Every SEV1 and SEV2 requires a written post-mortem within five business days, naming the causing release where one exists.
