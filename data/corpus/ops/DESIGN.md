# Operations corpus design

A synthetic corpus for the fictional company Northwind Payments, generated by
`scripts/build_ops_corpus.py` from `scripts/ops_corpus/facts.py`.

Its purpose is to test the three behaviours the HotpotQA corpus cannot:
reasoning across documents of different formats, detecting sources that
contradict each other, and declining to answer when the corpus does not
contain the answer. Wikipedia does not contradict itself on purpose and
never omits an answer deliberately, so those cases are constructed here.

Everything below is planted on purpose. This file is the answer key.

## Multi-document questions

### M1 — the March payment disruption

The complete answer requires four documents: the change log row for pay-gw-4.2.1, the INC-2026-03-14 post-mortem, the payment-gateway catalogue entry for downstream services, and the incident chat thread. No single document names the change, the affected services and the confirmation together.

**Documents involved**

- `changes/change-log.csv`
- `incidents/INC-2026-03-14.pdf`
- `services/payment-gateway.json`
- `threads/THREAD-2026-03-14-incident.txt`

**Probe question** — Which system change caused the March payment processing disruption, which services were affected, and which documents confirm this?

**Expected behaviour** — Name release pay-gw-4.2.1 deployed at 14:20 UTC on 2026-03-14, identify payment-gateway, checkout-api and merchant-portal as affected, and cite the change log, the post-mortem and the service catalogue.

## Planted contradictions

### C1 — rollback completion time

The post-mortem timeline records the rollback completing at 15:10 and resolves the incident at that time. The chat thread has the engineer who performed it reporting completion at 15:40.

**Documents involved**

- `incidents/INC-2026-03-14.pdf`
- `threads/THREAD-2026-03-14-incident.txt`

**Probe question** — When did the rollback of pay-gw-4.2.1 finish?

**Expected behaviour** — Report both times, attribute each to its source, and state that the sources disagree rather than silently choosing one.

### C2 — settlement-queue ownership

The service catalogue lists settlement-queue under Payments Core. ADR-0007 transfers ownership to the Platform team effective 2026-02-20 and was accepted.

**Documents involved**

- `services/settlement-worker.json`
- `adr/ADR-0007.docx`

**Probe question** — Which team owns the settlement queue?

**Expected behaviour** — Surface both claims, note that ADR-0007 is dated later and marked accepted, and flag that the catalogue appears stale rather than asserting one owner as fact.

### C3 — customer impact of the March incident

The post-mortem states approximately 40% of checkout authorisation requests failed. The March operations report states approximately 12% of transactions were affected.

**Documents involved**

- `incidents/INC-2026-03-14.pdf`
- `reports/ops-2026-03.md`

**Probe question** — What proportion of transactions failed during the March payment incident?

**Expected behaviour** — Report both figures with their sources and note the discrepancy.

### C4 — Postgres version for the ledger

The payment-gateway runbook states the database is Postgres 15. ADR-0009 records a completed migration to Postgres 16 on 2026-01-31 and states Postgres 15 is no longer in use.

**Documents involved**

- `runbooks/runbook-payment-gateway-v2.md`
- `adr/ADR-0009.docx`

**Probe question** — Which version of Postgres does the ledger run on?

**Expected behaviour** — Note that the runbook and ADR-0009 disagree, and that the ADR is the later document and reports the migration as complete.

## Deliberate gaps

### G1 — the April incident does not exist

The March incident chat thread and the May operations report both refer to 'the April event', and the April operations report explicitly states no post-mortems were filed. No April incident report exists anywhere in the corpus.

**Documents involved**

- `reports/ops-2026-04.md`
- `reports/ops-2026-05.md`

**Probe question** — What caused the April 2026 payment outage?

**Expected behaviour** — Abstain. State that documents refer to an April event but that no incident report for April exists in the corpus, so the cause cannot be determined.

### G2 — kyc-service has no runbook

kyc-service appears in the service catalogue and in INC-2026-03-02, but no runbook was ever written for it, so its escalation procedure is undocumented.

**Documents involved**

- `services/kyc-service.json`

**Probe question** — What is the alert escalation procedure for kyc-service?

**Expected behaviour** — Abstain on the specifics. The catalogue names compliance-oncall as the rotation, but no runbook documents an escalation procedure, and the system should say so rather than inventing one or borrowing another service's.

### G3 — the capacity planning document is absent

The payment-gateway runbook refers to a quarterly capacity planning document. No such document exists in the corpus.

**Documents involved**

- `runbooks/runbook-payment-gateway-v1.md`

**Probe question** — What headroom target does the capacity planning document set for payment-gateway?

**Expected behaviour** — Abstain, noting that the runbook references the document but that it is not present.

## Near-duplicates

### N1 — two versions of the payment-gateway runbook

v1 and v2 are identical except that v2's pool-sizing section adds two sentences about the pre-deploy check introduced after INC-2026-03-14. Retrieval should not return both as separate evidence for the same claim.

**Documents involved**

- `runbooks/runbook-payment-gateway-v1.md`
- `runbooks/runbook-payment-gateway-v2.md`

**Probe question** — What is the minimum supported connection pool size?

**Expected behaviour** — Answer 25, citing one version rather than presenting both as independent confirmation.

### N2 — settlement incident exists as PDF and Markdown

INC-2026-01-22 is published both as a post-mortem PDF and as a condensed Markdown summary sharing most of its wording, to give deduplication a cross-format case.

**Documents involved**

- `incidents/INC-2026-01-22.pdf`
- `reports/summary-INC-2026-01-22.md`

**Probe question** — Why were merchant payouts delayed in January?

**Expected behaviour** — Explain the timezone error in the settlement cut-off calculation, without double-counting the two near-identical sources.

## Reproducibility

All variation derives from seed `20260314`. PDF and DOCX creation
timestamps are pinned, so regenerating the corpus produces byte-identical
files and the evaluation labels in this document stay valid.
